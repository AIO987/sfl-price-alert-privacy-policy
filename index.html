Privacy Policy

SFL Price Alert · Effective date: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SFL Price Alert (“the App”) handles information when you use the application. The App is an independent companion application for viewing Sunflower Land market resource prices, configuring price alerts, and monitoring farm-related resources. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sunflower Land.

1. Information the App processes

Depending on the features you use and the App version, the App and its services may process:

  • Device and app identifiers: an app-generated device identifier and a server-issued authentication token or token hash, used to recognize the installation and maintain its connection to the service.
  • Push notification information: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration token and notification-related data, used to deliver price-alert notifications.
  • App settings and alert data: resource selections, alert prices, alert status, and related settings needed to provide the alert service.
  • Farm-related information: a farm identifier or resource information that you choose to submit, used to retrieve or display farm-related data.
  • Technical information: connection events, timestamps, app version, and diagnostic or error information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.
  • Advertising information: in the free version, Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may process advertising identifiers, device information, and ad interaction data to provide and measure ads, subject to Google's settings and policies.

The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, postal address, or phone number to use its core features. Do not submit information that you do not want processed.

2. How information is used

Information is used to:

  • display market prices and resource information;
  • create, synchronize, and evaluate price alerts;
  • send push notifications when alert conditions are met;
  • provide farm-related resource monitoring when requested;
  • maintain service availability, prevent abuse, and diagnose technical issues;
  • display and measure advertising in the free version of the App.

3. Service providers and sharing

The App uses third-party services that may process information under their own privacy terms:

  • Google Firebase / Firebase Cloud Messaging for app infrastructure and push notifications: Firebase Privacy and Security.
  • Google AdMob to provide advertising in the free version: Google advertising policies and Google Privacy Policy.

Information may also be processed by the App's server and hosting providers to operate the features described in this Policy. We do not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except as needed to provide the App, protect the service, comply with law, or with your direction.

4. Advertising and paid version

The free version may display ads provided by Google AdMob. The paid version does not intentionally display in-app advertisements. Advertising providers may use cookies, mobile advertising identifiers, or similar technologies as described in their own policies. You can manage advertising personalization and reset or delete your advertising ID through your Android device settings, where available.

5. Data retention and deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Alert settings and related service data may be retained while they are needed for the alert functionality. Diagnostic logs may be retained for a limited operational period.

You can remove alerts and change submitted settings in the App. To request deletion of information associated with your installation, contact the developer using the address below and include enough information to identify the installation. We may need to retain certain records where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

6. Data security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the developer so the matter can be reviewed.

8. International processing

Information may be processed on servers located in countries other than your own, depending on the infrastructure and service providers used. Those countries may have different data protection laws.

9. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated when the App, its services, or applicable legal requirements change. The revised version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date.
